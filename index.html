Bridging Human Compassion & Technical Frontier

An independent academic research group dedicated to exploring humanistic rehabilitation for sub-clinical mental health conditions and evaluating the safety alignment of Large Language Models (LLMs) in psychosocial care.

Our Core Research Architecture

Investigating the intersection of psychological resilience, computational alignment, and bioethical governance to foster a safer digital society.

🌱

Sub-clinical Mental Health

Addressing the "gray zone" of mental health: burnout, prolonged stress, languishing, and mild anxiety. We focus on early identification and low-friction micro-interventions before symptoms escalate to clinical thresholds.
🤖

AI in Psychosocial Therapy

Analyzing the dual-edged sword of Generative AI. We evaluate how frontier LLMs democratize cognitive behavioral structuring while mapping out hard architectural redlines against crisis hallucinations and deceptive empathy.
🧭

Methodology & Open Toolkits

Providing standardized psycholinguistic feature ontologies (PFO-v2), four-tier crisis triage protocols, open safety benchmarks (SADB-2026), and verified non-anthropomorphic CBT/ACT system prompts.
⚖️

Ethical Charter & Governance

Upholding the HCRI Charter for Algorithmic Care, including the 50% minimum human touch quota, zero confessional data monetization, and interdisciplinary IRB bioethical oversight.
🔬

Investigators & API Protocol

Meet our interdisciplinary research leads and review our frontier LLM API compute specifications, multi-turn red-teaming methodologies, and non-commercial open-science disclosures.
