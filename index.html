Bridging Human Compassion & Technical Frontier

An independent research group dedicated to exploring humanistic rehabilitation for sub-clinical mental health conditions and evaluating the profound impacts of Large Language Models (LLMs) on modern psychosocial care.

Our Core Research Domains

Investigating the intersection of psychological resilience and computational alignment to foster a healthier digital society.

🌱

Sub-clinical Mental Health

Addressing the "gray zone" of mental health: burnout, prolonged stress, languishing, and mild anxiety. We focus on early intervention, destigmatization, and holistic rehabilitation frameworks before symptoms escalate to clinical thresholds.
🤖

AI in Psychosocial Therapy

Analyzing the dual-edged sword of Generative AI. We research how LLMs democratize access to cognitive behavioral support, while rigorously mapping out ethical guardrails against emotional exploitation, hallucinations, and privacy risks.

Open Collaboration

We invite researchers, clinicians, and AI alignment engineers to collaborate on developing robust frameworks for technology-assisted humanistic care.
