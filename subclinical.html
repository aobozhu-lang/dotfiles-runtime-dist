LLMs in Psychological Care

Evaluating the empirical benefits of computational empathy while constructing strict ethical guardrails against algorithmic risks.

The Dual-Edged Paradigm

Generative Large Language Models (LLMs) are transforming psychosocial support. Our research isolates where AI serves as a powerful catalyst for healing, and where it poses systemic dangers.

Positive Catalyst

Democratizing Access to CBT

LLMs excel at guiding users through structured Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) exercises. By identifying cognitive distortions and offering reframing techniques 24/7, AI acts as an accessible bridge for those facing financial or geographical barriers to human therapists.

Positive Catalyst

Continuous Sub-clinical Monitoring

Through longitudinal sentiment analysis of user interactions, AI models can detect subtle semantic shifts indicating escalating anxiety or depressive onset, enabling preemptive alerts and early intervention routing before a crisis occurs.

Positive Catalyst

Reducing Clinician Burnout

Automating clinical intake, empathetic summarization of patient histories, and drafting initial care plans allows human psychiatrists and social workers to reclaim their most critical asset: unhurried, face-to-face empathetic presence.
