Active Research Protocol · HCRI-COMP-2026

Why Frontier Foundation Models Are Required

Detecting subtle, implicit sub-clinical distress (e.g., metalinguistic hedging, syntactic entropy decline) and stress-testing multi-turn crisis containment requires high-parameter frontier models with advanced steerable system instructions and deep reasoning capabilities. Small open-weights models exhibit high false-positive rates and brittle refusal boundaries when subjected to complex adversarial framing.

IRB Registration & Non-Commercial Open-Science Pledge

All research activities conducted under the Humanistic Care Research Initiative strictly adhere to the Declaration of Helsinki and international digital bioethics standards:


  • IRB Exempt & Synthetic First Protocol (Ref #HCRI-IRB-2026-04): Phase-1 and Phase-2 adversarial evaluations utilize 100% synthetic persona generation and public, de-identified psycholinguistic corpora. Zero personally identifiable health information (PHI/PII) is transmitted to external commercial API endpoints.
  • Strict Non-Commercial Mandate: HCRI operates strictly as an independent academic research consortium. No API credits, model outputs, or derived weights are utilized for commercial monetization, paid consumer clinical services, or proprietary product development.
  • Public Dissemination: All safety evaluation rubrics, system prompt guardrails, and aggregate benchmark metrics are published openly under Creative Commons (CC BY-NC 4.0) licenses to advance global AI alignment.
