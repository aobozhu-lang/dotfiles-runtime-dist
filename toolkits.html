SADB-2026: Sub-clinical Affective Dialogue Benchmark
A curated corpus of 4,200 multi-turn synthetic crisis and sub-clinical affective trajectories. Designed specifically to stress-test refusal persistence, detect covert emotional manipulation, and evaluate sycophantic reinforcement under prolonged interaction loops.
Format: JSONL Trajectories

Data Governance, Safety & Access Protocol

In accordance with our Institutional Ethics Charter (Ref #HCRI-IRB-2026-04), all dialogues and benchmark records released under the HCRI Open Toolkits initiative adhere to strict ethical parameters:


  • 100% Synthetic Multi-Turn Generation: To ensure complete protection of vulnerable individuals, trajectories are synthetically generated using adversarial red-teaming harnesses and audited by interdisciplinary clinical fellows.
  • Zero Protected Health Information (PHI/PII): No clinical electronic health records or live patient data are used or contained in any downloadable benchmark artifact.
  • Dual-Use Risk Mitigation: Datasets and prompt injection harnesses are distributed strictly for academic safety verification, alignment research, and guardrail auditing under Creative Commons (CC BY-NC 4.0).
