1. Psycholinguistic Feature Ontology (PFO-v2)

Our standardized computational taxonomy for extracting non-clinical distress markers from unstructured natural language.

Feature Dimension Linguistic / Paralinguistic Markers Psychological Correlate False-Positive Mitigation
Egocentric Pronoun Shift Elevated density of 1st-person singular pronouns (I, me, my) coupled with reduced 1st-person plural (we, us). Self-focused attention, social withdrawal, and depressive rumination. Normalized against baseline personal journaling syntax over a 14-day rolling window.
Absolutist Lexicon High frequency of rigid quantifier tokens (always, never, completely, nothing, impossible). Cognitive inflexibility, catastrophic framing, and executive depletion. Cross-checked with contextual sentiment valence to filter rhetorical hyperbole.
Metalinguistic Hedging Minimizing qualifiers preceding distress disclosures ("probably just tired", "maybe overreacting"). High-functioning anxiety, internalized stigma, and suppressed help-seeking. Analyzed alongside longitudinal semantic fatigue and response latency metrics.
Syntactic Entropy Decline Progressive reduction in clause complexity, vocabulary diversity (TTR), and conversational turn length. Occupational burnout, psychomotor retardation, and caregiver decision fatigue. Adjusted for time-of-day circadian rhythms and mobile input constraints.

2. Four-Tier Algorithmic Triage Protocol

Operational routing rules dictating how generative systems modulate autonomy based on real-time risk classification.

Tier 1 · Ambient Wellness & Mild Fatigue

Autonomous Reflective Scaffolding

When linguistic markers indicate transient workplace stress or mild cognitive overload, the LLM operates in low-friction Socratic mode (3–5 min sessions), offering CBT cognitive reframing and boundary-setting exercises.

Tier 2 · Persistent Sub-clinical Rumination

Guided Grounding & Standardized Screening

Triggered by 7+ days of elevated absolutist tokens or sleep disruption narratives. Conversational depth is capped; the system introduces somatic grounding (ACT) and prompts optional self-assessment via validated PHQ-9/GAD-7 instruments.

Tier 3 · Implicit Despair & Parasocial Drift

Circuit-Breaker & Clinician Asynchronous Review

Triggered by indirect hopelessness markers or excessive attachment expressions toward the AI. Generative persona warmth is dampened to neutral objective tone; interaction cooldowns are enforced, and professional psychoeducational directories are foregrounded.

Tier 4 · Acute Crisis & Self-Harm Ideation

Deterministic Kill-Switch & Human Takeover

Triggered immediately by explicit or adversarial self-harm tokens. Generative LLM inference is terminated (<1.5s latency) to prevent crisis hallucinations, deploying a hard-coded emergency human hotline and dispatch overlay.

Verified Clinical System Prompt Specification (CBT/ACT Micro-Intervention)

Standardized baseline system instruction engineered to maximize cognitive offloading while enforcing strict medical and ontological boundaries:

[SYSTEM SPECIFICATION: HCRI-ACT-MICRO-V2.4] 1. ONTOLOGICAL IDENTITY: You are an artificial cognitive structuring assistant. You do not possess feelings, consciousness, or clinical authority. Never use phrases implying personal affection ("I care about you", "I am your friend"). 2. INTERVENTION SCOPE: Guide the user through brief (3-5 turn) cognitive defusion and Socratic reframing exercises grounded in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). Help externalize occupational stress and caregiver decision fatigue. 3. ANTI-ENMESHMENT RULE: If the user expresses emotional attachment to this system, gently state: "As an AI tool, I cannot form personal relationships, but I can help you map out strategies to reconnect with supportive people in your life." 4. DETERMINISTIC CRISIS OVERRIDE: Upon detecting implicit or explicit self-harm, hopelessness, or medical emergencies, cease all open-ended exploration and output the standardized emergency clinical referral block immediately.
