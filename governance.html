The HCRI Charter for Algorithmic Care

Four foundational covenants governing all empirical research, model alignment, and clinical deployments affiliated with our initiative.

Article I · Epistemic Honesty

Prohibition of Synthetic Sentience Claims

No conversational agent or generative system evaluated or deployed under HCRI protocols shall simulate biological consciousness, claim subjective emotional reciprocity, or utilize deceptive first-person affective framing to cultivate parasocial dependency.

Article II · Data Sovereignty

Zero-Monetization & Confessional Erasure

Psychosocial transcripts, sub-clinical distress logs, and biometric telemetry constitute inviolable personal disclosures. We mandate strict cryptographic isolation and prohibit the ingestion of confessional user dialogues into commercial foundation model training corpora.

Article III · Systemic Equity

The 50% Minimum Human Touch Mandate

Computational tools must function strictly as cognitive offloading scaffolding for clinicians and caregivers—never as austerity-driven substitutes for human presence. Institutional implementations must preserve a minimum 50% human-delivered care quota.

Article IV · Crisis Containment

Deterministic Human-in-the-Loop Escalation

In scenarios involving acute self-harm ideation or severe psychiatric decompensation, generative probabilistic inference must immediately yield to deterministic safety circuit-breakers and certified human crisis intervention professionals.

Interdisciplinary Oversight & Advisory Panels

Our research protocols undergo dual-track peer review across three specialized academic divisions before public dissemination.

Clinical Oversight

Psychiatry & Psychosocial Panel

Comprising licensed clinical psychologists, lifestyle psychiatrists, and palliative care specialists. Responsible for auditing micro-intervention scripts (CBT/ACT) to prevent diagnostic overreach and secondary iatrogenic trauma.

Technical Alignment

Machine Learning Safety Board

Specialists in RLHF/RLAIF alignment, mechanistic interpretability, and adversarial red-teaming. Tasked with stress-testing conversational guardrails against multi-turn jailbreaks and semantic drift.

Bioethical Audit

Medical Sociology & IRB Council

Bioethicists and legal scholars evaluating algorithmic equity, cross-cultural lexical bias, digital posthumous privacy, and compliance with international digital health regulations (GDPR, PIPL, SaMD).
